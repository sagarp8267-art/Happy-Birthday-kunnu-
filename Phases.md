# Development Phases

## Phase 0 — Setup
HTML5 + CSS3 + Vanilla JS modular architecture and assets.

## Phase 1 — Password
Birthday/date unlock and playful wrong-password response.

## Phase 2 — Heart + Tree Intro
Heart → birthday text → tree → flowers → final birthday sequence.

## Phase 3 — Balloons
Premium floating balloon celebration.

## Phase 4 — Funny Companion
Recurring companion system and introduction.

## Phase 5 — 3D Scrapbook
Physical scrapbook, binding, photos, stickers and page flip.

## Phase 6 — Favourite Idiot
Funny/romantic relationship reasons and reactions.

## Phase 7 — 3D Photo World
Floating photos, depth, parallax and touch.

## Phase 8 — Boyfriend Quiz
Interactive quiz and funny reactions.

## Phase 9 — Boyfriend Awards
Animated awards and companion reactions.

## Phase 10 — Interactive 3D Cake
3D environment → unlit candles → user lights candles → flame/glow → make a wish → user cuts cake by swipe/click/drag → cake-cut animation → confetti → companion reaction → transition to letter.

## Phase 11 — Romantic Letter
Candle/smoke transition and romantic text reveal.

## Phase 12 — Future Together
Interactive romantic future scenes.

## Phase 13 — Final Surprise
Interactive gift opening and reveal.

## Phase 14 — Grand Finale
Final birthday celebration and emotional ending.

## Phase 15 — Audio + Transitions
Music, sound effects, mute/volume and transition polish.

## Phase 16 — Final QA
Test every scene, interaction, transition, responsive layout, assets and performance.

## Rule
Do not start the next phase until the current phase is tested.
