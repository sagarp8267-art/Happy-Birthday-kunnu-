# Design Specification

## Direction
Premium cinematic birthday experience combining romance, humor, interactive storytelling, 3D depth and a recurring funny companion.

## Story
It should feel like one continuous journey, not unrelated pages.

## Scene Design
- Password: minimal and mysterious.
- Intro: glowing heart → birthday/tree transformation.
- Balloons: celebratory and energetic.
- Companion: cute/funny contextual animations.
- Scrapbook: physical paper, binding, shadows and page depth.
- Photo World: dreamy 3D floating memories.
- Quiz: playful and game-like.
- Awards: premium achievement cards.
- Cake: emotional interaction peak.
- Letter: calm and emotional.
- Future: dreamy.
- Finale: gift reveal and birthday message.

## Cake Interaction
Night/city-like environment with cake centered.
Candles begin unlit. User lights them; flame glow appears. Show “Make a Wish”. User then cuts the cake with click/swipe/drag. Cake visibly separates/cuts, followed by confetti/particles and companion reaction. Transition from candle/smoke into the romantic letter.

## Motion
Slow emotional reveals, faster playful motion, smooth transitions and readable animation.

## Responsive / Performance
Touch-first interaction with desktop enhancement. Optimize large images, lazy-load assets and prefer GPU-friendly transforms.

## Accessibility
Readable text, touch-friendly controls and reduced-motion support where practical.
